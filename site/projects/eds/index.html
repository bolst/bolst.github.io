Emergency Dispatch Simulator

Training platform for 911 operators: live, two-way voice calls with AI callers over WebSockets, then a review scored from the transcript. Built in one weekend by a team of 4 for the Higgs Hackathon, where it placed top 6 of 80+ teams.
